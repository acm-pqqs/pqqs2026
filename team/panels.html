Panels

The Transition to Quantum-Safe Security: Technical and Societal Challenges

Panelists

Dr. Dustin Moody

Affiliation: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)

Biography: Dr. Dustin Moody is a mathematician in the Computer Security Division of the National Institute of Standards and Technology. Dr. Moody leads the Post-Quantum Cryptography project at NIST. He received his Ph.D. from the University of Washington in 2009. His area of research deals with elliptic curves, and their applications in cryptography.

Dr. Michele Mosca

Affiliation: Institute for Quantum Computing, University of Waterloo; evolutionQ

Biography: Dr. Michele Mosca is a pioneer in quantum computing and cybersecurity who has helped shape the global quantum and quantum-safe technology ecosystem. He initiated the quantum computing effort at the University of Waterloo in 1999 and was a principal architect of its development into the Institute for Quantum Computing (IQC), helping establish one of the world’s leading quantum research and innovation ecosystems. He is a founding member of Perimeter Institute and co-founder and CEO of evolutionQ. His work spans foundational quantum computing research, quantum-safe cybersecurity, talent development, commercialization, standards leadership, and international policy engagement. He has helped create or grow initiatives including Open Quantum Safe, the ETSI-IQC Quantum-Safe Cryptography Conference, Quantum-Safe Canada, Quantum Industry Canada, QCSYS/QSYS, and CryptoWorks21.
